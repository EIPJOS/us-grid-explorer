Infrastructure · Definition

Power plant

A facility containing one or more generators and associated equipment used to produce electricity.

In plain languageA facility containing one or more generators and associated equipment used to produce electricity.
Category
Infrastructure
Primary reference
U.S. Energy Information Administration

Why it matters

How this term helps you read grid data

Plant-level records group generators at a site. Fuel, status, and capacity can differ among generators within the same plant.

Example

Power plant in practice

A power plant profile may show both operating and proposed generators under one EIA plant code.
