Independent system operator (ISO)

An independent organization that operates a regional transmission system and may administer wholesale electricity markets.

In plain languageAn independent organization that operates a regional transmission system and may administer wholesale electricity markets.
Category
Markets and operators
Primary reference
Federal Energy Regulatory Commission

Why it matters

How this term helps you read grid data

ISOs coordinate grid operations across multiple utilities, helping manage transmission access, reliability, and regional market dispatch.
